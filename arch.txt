* using SDL time for dft
* using a queue:
  The decoder will add pcm bugger in the queue with the time-stamp
  The fft gen will pop form the queue until the top intersects with the current time
  current time will be given by sdl, giving the current time of audio playing
  in the end of the loop, the pcm buffer will be queue/handed-over to sdl to play
